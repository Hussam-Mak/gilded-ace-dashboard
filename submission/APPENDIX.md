# Claims and evidence appendix

Gilded Ace · TAMU Datathon 2026 · Dashboard Royale

Every claim in the write-up, traced to the table or query that produced it, the result, the competing explanation we tested, and what the evidence cannot show. Numbers are reproducible: clone the organizers' data next to this repository and run, in order, `analysis/forecast.py`, `analysis/checks.py`, `analysis/plan_options.py` and `analysis/claims_check.py`. Outputs are saved in `analysis/*_results.json`; "C1"… refer to blocks in `claims_check_results.json`.

Type: **Fact** = read directly from the data. **Estimate** = depends on a method with stated limits. **Assumption** = chosen by us, not measured.
